# Habits — marketing kit

**Angle:** every habit app trusts a tap. BeetFit asks for proof: the camera has to see the toothbrush, the glass, the vitamins.

**Tagline:** Habits that need proof.
**One-liner:** Set a time. When it rings, show Beet the thing. No camera, no tick.

## Store text (short description, ≤80 chars)
Fitness plan, food log and habits you prove on camera. On-device, private.

## Store "What's new"
New: Habits that need proof. Pick a time, and when it rings, show the camera your toothbrush, water or vitamins and it ticks itself off. Pick the days it runs on, keep a streak that forgives one slip, and your video never leaves your phone.

## Rules for copy
- Say "proof", "show", "prove"; avoid "AI magic".
- Always pair the camera with privacy: checked on the phone, video never uploaded (true: MediaPipe runs on-device).
- Don't promise an alarm that wakes a locked phone: on the web it's the system notification sound; the in-app ring needs the app open.
